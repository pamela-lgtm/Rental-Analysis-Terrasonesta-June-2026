The Solterra Long-Term Market

How your community is performing for long-term rentals

Solterra is a guard-gated resort community built primarily around short-term vacation rentals. That shapes the long-term picture in two ways worth understanding plainly.

First, the long-term unfurnished tenant pool inside a resort is more selective and somewhat narrower than in a standard residential subdivision — fewer renters are seeking a year-round home inside a vacation destination. Second, that same dynamic is exactly why the unfurnished long-term lane is a real opportunity: it is currently the larger share of long-term listings in the area (roughly seven in ten), and it is the specific niche we specialize in.

Across comparable four-bedroom leases in Solterra and the immediate Davenport corridor over the last several months, leased rents have ranged from $2,200 to $3,000, with a median of $2,500. Just as important as price is pace: these homes took an average of 71 days to lease, with a median of 79. That is the market's default speed at typical pricing and condition — and it is the number our approach is built to beat.

Pool & Pest Maintenance

Keeping quality in your hands, not the tenant's

You're right to want pool and pest care controlled rather than delegated to a tenant — that instinct protects the asset and aligns perfectly with how we operate. Here's how we recommend structuring it.

Retain the service; we coordinate the vendors

We engage and oversee your preferred pool and pest providers directly, so the work is done on schedule and to standard, and never depends on a tenant's diligence. The service costs themselves — roughly $100/month pool and $30/month pest — remain owner expenses, as vendor costs always are.

Build it into one clean rent number

Rather than itemizing $130 in monthly add-on fees on top of rent, we recommend folding that cost into a single, market-competitive rent figure. A tenant compares one number against every other listing; visible mandatory surcharges raise the effective cost, invite friction, and slow leasing — the opposite of our 21-day goal. One number keeps you competitive while you retain full control of the service.

What's included under management

Coordination and oversight of these vendors falls under our management service; the specific line-item inclusions for your selected plan will be confirmed with you in writing before anything is finalized.

Transition & Show-Ready Path

From short-term program to a 21-day lease

We completely understand the 60-day notice required to wind your property out of its short-term program — that timeline is yours to set, and we'll plan around it.

One thing we want to be transparent about up front, because it's central to how we deliver results: we don't list or show a property while it's still operating as a furnished short-term rental. A home shown furnished, between guest bookings, can't be presented as the unfurnished long-term residence it will become — and showing it that way would undercut both the rent and the speed we're aiming for. Our 21-day clock, and the rent figures above, begin the day the property is unfurnished and show-ready.

So the sequence we'd map together looks like this:

Give notice to the short-term program

Your 60-day window begins; we align our preparation to your target vacancy date.

Fully vacate & de-furnish

The property is cleared, deep-cleaned, and returned to an unfurnished long-term presentation.

Show-ready prep & professional marketing

We confirm the property meets our Show-Ready Standard, then capture professional photography and build the listing.

Launch — and the 21-day target begins

The property goes live unfurnished, priced to the market, and the placement clock starts.

Why This Works

The standard is the strategy

Your neighbor's 79 days at full price isn't a fluke — it's what happens when a sound property lists without the discipline that moves it quickly. Our approach is simple and it is the reason we hold every property to a show-ready, unfurnished standard before we begin:

  • Show-ready condition self-selects for a well-qualified, respectful tenant — the renter who walks into a clearly cared-for property and treats it that way.
  • Disciplined, market-true pricing — launching at $2,350 with a pre-planned adjustment if the first week's traffic is soft — keeps you ahead of the homes that sit.
  • Professional presentation and active marketing put your property in front of the right tenant pool from day one.

This is a partnership, not a transaction — and it's why we're intentionally selective about the properties we take on. When the foundation is right, the result you're asking for follows.
